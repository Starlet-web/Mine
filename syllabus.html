CSIR NET Life Science Syllabus 2027

📅 Examination Year: 2027  |  Subject Code: 705  |  Total Units: 13  |  Download PDF
Exam NameCSIR NET (National Eligibility Test)
SubjectLife Science
Exam ModeComputer Based Test (CBT)
PartsPart A (General Aptitude), Part B, Part C
Total Marks200

1 MOLECULES AND THEIR INTERACTION RELEVANT TO BIOLOGY

1.1 Structure & Bonds

  • Atomic structure, isotopes, covalent / non-covalent bonds (hydrogen, ionic, van der Waals, hydrophobic)
  • Water: unique properties, pH, buffers, Henderson-Hasselbalch equation
  • Stereochemistry: chirality, optical isomerism, enantiomers, diastereomers, racemic mixtures

1.2 Biomolecules

  • Carbohydrates: Monosaccharides, disaccharides, polysaccharides (starch, glycogen, cellulose, chitin), glycosaminoglycans
  • Lipids: Fatty acids, triacylglycerols, phospholipids, sphingolipids, cholesterol, membranes
  • Amino acids & Proteins: Classification, peptide bond, levels of organization, Ramachandran plot, domains, motifs
  • Nucleotides: DNA & RNA structure, A/B/Z forms, base pairing, Chargaff rule

1.3 Techniques

  • Chromatography (TLC, ion-exchange, affinity, HPLC, FPLC)
  • Electrophoresis (SDS-PAGE, agarose, 2D), centrifugation
  • Spectroscopy (UV-Vis, fluorescence, circular dichroism, NMR basics)
  • X-ray crystallography, cryo-EM introduction

2 CELLULAR ORGANIZATION

2.1 Membrane & Transport

  • Fluid mosaic model, lipid rafts, membrane proteins
  • Passive/active transport, ion channels (Na⁺/K⁺ ATPase), aquaporins
  • Endocytosis (phagocytosis, pinocytosis), exocytosis

2.2 Organelles & Cytoskeleton

  • Nucleus, ER, Golgi, lysosomes, peroxisomes, mitochondria, chloroplasts
  • Microfilaments (actin), microtubules (tubulin), intermediate filaments
  • Motor proteins: kinesin, dynein; cell motility

2.3 Cell Cycle & Division

  • Phases of cell cycle, cyclins, CDKs, checkpoints (G1/S, G2/M)
  • Mitosis & meiosis, synaptonemal complex, recombination
  • Apoptosis (intrinsic/extrinsic pathways), necrosis

3 FUNDAMENTAL PROCESSES

3.1 DNA Replication & Repair

  • Prokaryotic & eukaryotic replication machinery (DNA polymerases, helicase, primase, ligase)
  • Telomeres, telomerase. Replication fidelity
  • DNA repair: BER, NER, mismatch repair, homologous recombination, NHEJ

3.2 Transcription & Processing

  • Prokaryotic RNA polymerase, sigma factors. Eukaryotic RNA Pol I, II, III
  • Promoters, enhancers, transcription factors. mRNA capping, splicing, polyadenylation
  • Alternative splicing, RNA editing

3.3 Translation

  • Genetic code features. Ribosome structure, tRNA charging, initiation/elongation/termination
  • Post-translational modifications, protein targeting (signal peptide, SRP)

4 CELL COMMUNICATION AND CELL SIGNALING

4.1 Signaling Molecules & Receptors

  • Hormones, neurotransmitters, growth factors. GPCRs, RTKs, cytokine receptors
  • Second messengers: cAMP, IP3, DAG, calcium

4.2 Major Pathways

  • MAP kinase, JAK-STAT, PI3K-Akt, Wnt, Hedgehog, Notch, TGF-β
  • Apoptotic signaling, NF-κB pathway

5 DEVELOPMENTAL BIOLOGY

5.1 Gametogenesis & Fertilization

  • Spermatogenesis, oogenesis, acrosome reaction, cortical reaction, egg activation

5.2 Early Development

  • Cleavage, blastula, gastrulation in sea urchin, frog, chick, mouse
  • Organizers (Spemann-Mangold), embryonic induction

5.3 Pattern Formation & Stem Cells

  • Drosophila: maternal effect genes, segmentation, homeotic genes (Hox)
  • Stem cells: embryonic, adult, induced pluripotent (iPSC)

6 SYSTEM PHYSIOLOGY – PLANTS

6.1 Photosynthesis & Respiration

  • Light reactions, photosystems, C3/C4/CAM, photorespiration
  • Glycolysis, TCA cycle, electron transport, oxidative phosphorylation

6.2 Plant Hormones & Development

  • Auxin, gibberellin, cytokinin, ABA, ethylene, brassinosteroids
  • Photoreceptors (phytochrome, cryptochrome), vernalization

6.3 Transport & Stress

  • Xylem/phloem transport, mineral nutrition. Abiotic stress responses

7 SYSTEM PHYSIOLOGY – ANIMALS

7.1 Cardiovascular & Respiratory

  • Heart, cardiac cycle, blood pressure. Gas exchange, hemoglobin

7.2 Nervous & Muscle

  • Action potential, synapse. Muscle contraction (sliding filament)

7.3 Excretory & Endocrine

  • Kidney function, osmoregulation. Major endocrine glands

8 INHERITANCE BIOLOGY

8.1 Mendelian & Extensions

  • Laws, gene interaction, epistasis, pleiotropy, polygenic inheritance

8.2 Linkage & Chromosomes

  • Crossing over, mapping, sex determination, chromosomal aberrations

8.3 Population Genetics

  • Hardy-Weinberg, genetic drift, gene flow, selection

9 DIVERSITY OF LIFE FORMS

9.1 Taxonomy & Evolution

  • Five kingdoms, cladistics, molecular phylogeny. Natural selection, speciation

9.2 Microbes & Plants

  • Bacteria, viruses, fungi. Algae, bryophytes, pteridophytes, gymnosperms, angiosperms

10 ECOLOGICAL PRINCIPLES

10.1 Ecosystem & Environment

  • Food chain, energy flow, biogeochemical cycles, biomes

10.2 Population & Community

  • Growth models, competition, predation, succession, biodiversity indices

11 EVOLUTION AND BEHAVIOUR

11.1 Evolutionary Concepts

  • Origin of life, molecular evolution, adaptive radiation, coevolution

11.2 Animal Behaviour

  • Innate/learned, communication, altruism, kin selection, sociobiology

12 APPLIED BIOLOGY

12.1 Biotechnological Tools

  • Recombinant DNA, restriction enzymes, cloning, PCR, blotting, CRISPR

12.2 Medical & Agriculture

  • Vaccines, monoclonal antibodies, transgenic plants (Bt cotton), gene therapy

13 METHODS IN BIOLOGY

13.1 Molecular & Imaging

  • Microscopy (fluorescence, confocal, electron). FACS, ELISA, RIA

13.2 Computational & Biostatistics

  • Mean, median, SD, t-test, chi-square. Bioinformatics tools (BLAST)
